\# Technical Research \& Source Protocol



\## Purpose



This protocol defines how technical information is researched, sourced,

verified, and transformed into educational content.



The goal is to build a durable educational library rather than content

optimized for short-term trends.



Every important technical claim in a lecture should have a traceable basis.



\---



\# 1. Core Research Principle



The channel follows this rule:



> Research first. Explain second. Simplify third. Never simplify into falsehood.



A source is not automatically trustworthy because it appears highly in

search results or is widely shared.



Source quality depends on:



\- Authority

\- Proximity to the original knowledge

\- Technical accuracy

\- Evidence

\- Recency where relevant

\- Reproducibility

\- Context



\---



\# 2. Source Hierarchy



Sources are classified into five tiers.



\## Tier 0 — Primary Sources



Highest-value sources for original technical claims.



Examples:



\- Original research papers

\- Original algorithms

\- Official specifications

\- Standards

\- Original mathematical publications

\- Official technical reports



Use when discussing:



\- Who introduced an algorithm

\- Original architecture

\- Experimental results

\- Research claims

\- Novel methods
