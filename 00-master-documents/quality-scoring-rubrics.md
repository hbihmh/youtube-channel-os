\# YouTube Technical Education — Quality Scoring Rubrics



\## Purpose



This document defines the quality gates that every technical lecture must

pass before it is published.



The goal is not perfection.



The goal is to ensure that every published lecture is:



\- Technically accurate

\- Mathematically sound

\- Reproducible

\- Pedagogically clear

\- Honest about limitations

\- Consistent with the curriculum

\- Valuable as a long-term educational resource



A lecture should never be published solely because it is finished.



It must be \*\*verified and ready to teach\*\*.
